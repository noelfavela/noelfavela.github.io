# Fire TV Stick Top Shelf

Fire TV Stick Top Shelf is a small personal plugin for the Projectivy launcher on my own Amazon Fire TV Stick. It shows Apple TV style "Top Shelf" wallpapers above the app dock: trending titles for my streaming apps, the next Formula 1 race, and the newest videos from the YouTube channels I subscribe to.

It is a personal, non-commercial project. It is not sold, distributed or offered to other users.
