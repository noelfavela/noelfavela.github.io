# Privacy Policy

Fire TV Stick Top Shelf is a personal, non-commercial plugin that runs only on my own Amazon Fire TV Stick. This policy explains what it does with data.

## Google / YouTube data

With my permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API with the read-only scope youtube.readonly. It reads the list of channels the signed-in account subscribes to and the recent public uploads of those channels, to show their newest videos as wallpapers on the TV home screen.

The app does not post, change or delete anything on the account, and it does not read email, contacts or any other Google data.

## Storage

The Google sign-in token and the generated wallpaper images are stored only on the Fire TV device itself. Nothing is sent to any server operated by me or any third party. Data is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

## Other data sources

The app also downloads public information from The Movie Database (TMDB) and the Jolpica Formula 1 API. No personal data is sent to them.

## Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by uninstalling the app from the Fire TV. Uninstalling deletes all stored data.

## Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
